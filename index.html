ExpenseFlow Privacy Policy

Understand how your information is handled.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ExpenseFlow, a personal expense tracking application, handles information when you use the app. Please read it before using the application.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, ExpenseFlow may process information you enter, such as transaction amounts, dates, titles, categories, notes, income and expense records, your username, avatar preference, and selected currency.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used to provide app features such as recording transactions, displaying balances, organizing expenses, preparing reports, and remembering your preferences.

3. Storage on Your Device

ExpenseFlow uses local app storage for information such as transactions and preferences. Information stored locally may be lost if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Device backup behavior depends on Android settings and the backup features you use.

4. Imports and Permissions

If you choose to import a supported file, the app may process its contents to help you review transaction information. If SMS import or other permission-based features are included in the version you use, access is subject to the relevant Android permissions and the disclosures shown in the app.

You can review or change app permissions in Android Settings.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Data handling may depend on the services and software libraries included in the version of ExpenseFlow you use. Before release, the developer should verify whether the app transmits information to external services, including backup, analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising providers, and disclose those practices accurately.

6. Security

ExpenseFlow relies on the app's implementation and Android's available security features to help protect stored information. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally generally remains until it is deleted through an available app feature, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Copies may remain if device backup or another storage mechanism is enabled.

8. Children's Privacy

ExpenseFlow is a personal finance utility. The developer should provide appropriate safeguards and handle children's information in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
